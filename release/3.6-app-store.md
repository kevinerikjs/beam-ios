## What's New in This Version

```
Encrypted connection: everything between Beam and your Mac is now encrypted with a key from your pairing. That covers the picture, the sound, and every click and key you type, so nobody else on your Wi-Fi can read it. Update Beacon on your Mac too (it updates itself).

Also: the Lock Screen widget now works on every iPhone Beam supports, and Beam no longer asks for camera access it doesn't use.
```

## Description

```
Beam puts your Mac's screen and sound on your iPhone or iPad.

Watch a render finish from the couch, keep a film going in bed, or play a Mac game with a controller from across the room. When something on the Mac needs you, tap the stream to click, drag, scroll and type.

• Your whole Mac display, or just one window, with Mac audio
• Up to 4K and your display's native resolution, up to 120 fps on ProMotion screens
• Picture in Picture, crop and zoom
• Click mode: tap to click, hold to right-click or drag, two fingers to scroll
• Live keyboard with esc, tab, arrows, function keys and ⌘ ⌥ ⌃ ⇧
• A bar of up to eight buttons you design on the Mac: shortcuts, media keys, macros
• Game controllers paired with your phone show up on the Mac as a real gamepad
• Encrypted end to end with the secret from pairing. No account, no Beam servers in between
• Open source (AGPL-3.0)

Free:
• Unlimited streaming for your first 3 days
• Then 30 minutes in every 24 hours, with every feature, on your home Wi-Fi

Beam Unlimited (one-time purchase):
• Stream as long as you like
• Stream from anywhere over your own Tailscale network

Needs the free Beacon app on your Mac (macOS 14 or later): beamscreen.app
```
