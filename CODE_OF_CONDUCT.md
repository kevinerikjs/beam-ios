# Code of Conduct

This is a small project maintained by one person, so this is short.

## The standard

Be decent to people. Assume the person you are replying to is acting in good faith and knows
things you do not. Criticise code, decisions, and ideas as sharply as you like. Do not criticise
people.

Specifically, none of the following are welcome here: harassment, personal attacks, insults about
someone's identity or background, sexualised language or imagery, deliberately publishing
someone's private information, or sustained disruption of discussion.

Blunt technical disagreement is fine and often useful. "This approach will break on lossy networks
and here is why" is a good comment. Someone being wrong about video codecs is not a character
flaw.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, and any other space in this project, and
it applies when you are representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to **[support@beamscreen.app](mailto:support@beamscreen.app)**. Reports go to the
maintainer, are kept private, and are not shared with the person reported unless that is necessary
to act on it.

There is no committee and no appeals board. The maintainer will read it, ask questions if the
situation is unclear, and decide. Depending on what happened, that means a warning, deleted
comments, or a block from the project. Serious or repeated behaviour gets a block without a
warning first.

If the problem is with the maintainer, saying so publicly in an issue is a legitimate move and
will not be held against you.
