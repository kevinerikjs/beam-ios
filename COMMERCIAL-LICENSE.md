# Commercial Licensing

Beam is dual licensed.

## The default: AGPL-3.0

Unless you have a separate written agreement, Beam is available to you under the
[GNU Affero General Public License v3.0](./LICENSE). That is the default for everyone, and for
the overwhelming majority of people it is all they will ever need.

Under the AGPL you may freely use, study, modify, and redistribute Beam, including for
commercial purposes. The AGPL does not restrict selling. What it requires is that if you
distribute Beam or a derivative of it, you release your version's complete source under the
AGPL as well.

In plain terms:

| What you want to do | AGPL is enough |
| --- | --- |
| Use Beam yourself, at home or at work | Yes |
| Modify it for your own use | Yes |
| Fork it and publish your fork under AGPL | Yes |
| Sell a fork whose full source you also publish under AGPL | Yes |
| Build on Beam while keeping your own source closed | No, see below |

## The commercial license

If you want to build on Beam **without** the AGPL's source disclosure obligations, for example
inside a closed source product, a proprietary appliance, or an app you ship under terms that
conflict with copyleft, you need a commercial license.

These are negotiable, and we would like to hear from you. Depending on what you are building, an
arrangement might be a one time fee, per seat, a revenue share, or something else entirely.

Get in touch and describe what you have in mind:

**[support@beamscreen.app](mailto:support@beamscreen.app)** with the subject `Commercial license`

Useful things to include: what you are building, roughly how it would be distributed, and whether
you need the whole project or just part of it. A short paragraph is fine to start.

## Some notes

- Commercial licenses are granted at our discretion. We are not obliged to offer one to everyone,
  and terms will vary with the use case.

  license for anyone else, and it does not make your changes private retroactively if you have
  already distributed them under the AGPL.
- Copyright in Beam is held by Kevin Erik Iin. This is what makes dual licensing possible: as

- Contributions are welcome, and we ask contributors to sign a short [CLA](./CLA.md) so that this
  arrangement keeps working. See that document for why.
- None of this page is legal advice, and it is a summary rather than a contract. Where this
  description and the [LICENSE](./LICENSE) text disagree, the LICENSE text governs. Any commercial
  license would be its own signed agreement.

## Trademarks

The AGPL covers the source code. It does not grant rights to the **Beam** or **Beacon** names,
logos, or app icons. You are welcome to fork the code under the AGPL, but please ship it under
your own name so users are not confused about who wrote and supports what.
