# Contributor License Agreement

## Why this exists

Beam is [dual licensed](./COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md): AGPL-3.0 for everyone by default, with a
separate commercial license available for people who need to build on it without the AGPL's
source disclosure obligations.

That arrangement only works while one party holds the rights to the whole codebase. If a
contribution is merged without an agreement in place, that contributor owns the copyright in
their lines, and from then on nobody can offer the project under any other terms, because you
cannot relicense code you do not own. A single unagreed merge would quietly remove the ability
to offer commercial licenses at all.

Keeping that ability is what lets Beam stay open and still pay for the time that goes into it,
which is why the agreement below exists and why it is one paragraph of substance rather than five
pages.

## How to sign

Add a line to the pull request description:

```
I have read the CLA and I agree to it.
```

There is no form and no separate signature, and you only need to do it once. If
you would rather not agree, that is completely fine, and you are still very welcome to open
issues, report bugs, and suggest ideas.
